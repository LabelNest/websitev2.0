A guided, stage and sector-aware system that takes founders and fund managers from zero to institutional-grade. Structured data room, legal checklist, investor fit scoring, and expert templates — built for emerging markets where institutional prep tools have never existed.

Get started

Founder Track

For startups raising pre-seed through Series B. Your company profile, team, traction, financials, and legal documents — structured once, shared in one clean link.

  • Structured investor-grade data room
  • Expert-curated pitch, financial, and legal templates
  • Investor fit scoring — matched by stage and sector
  • Per-field visibility controls — public, investor-only, private
  • Document vault — every file tagged and linked

Fund Track

For emerging GPs raising Fund I or Fund II from LPs. LP data room, fund thesis, terms, track record documentation, and LP fit scoring — all structured.

  • LP-ready data room — fund thesis, terms, track record
  • LP fit scoring — matched by mandate and geography
  • Fund documentation templates included

The SF standard: Silicon Valley investors expect a data room by the second meeting. No room means no meeting three — regardless of how good the pitch was. Most emerging market founders have never seen an institutional data room. Their reference point is a pitch deck and a WhatsApp message. A clean data room is one of the few signals that crosses every geographic and credibility barrier.

Where data supply meets
data demand

NestLens Exchange is the marketplace that connects teams who produce private-market data, research, and reports with teams who need them — without long procurement cycles or middlemen. Discovery is broken, procurement is slow, and trust is missing. Exchange fixes all three.

Start buying List as seller

For Sellers — Turn data into revenue

List datasets, custom research capabilities, and report offerings in front of funds, founders, advisors, and research teams actively looking.

  • List once, stay discoverable — clear scope, pricing, delivery terms
  • Respond to intent-driven buyer requests
  • Direct conversations — scope and close in-platform
  • Build a credible verified profile over time
  • Any format — raw data, custom research, recurring feeds, one-off reports

For Buyers — Source exactly what you need

Find vetted providers for datasets, custom research, and reports — or post exactly what you need and let qualified sellers come to you.

  • Skip the procurement drag — from need to deliverable fast
