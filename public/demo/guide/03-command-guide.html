01 The promise in one minute

Command is the one place where a fund runs itself. Pipeline, committee, portfolio, LPs and compliance sit together, fed by Atlas and by the founders’ own data rooms, and every AI draft waits for a person to approve it.

The problem it removes

Deals live in email and spreadsheets

Nobody can say which deal is stuck, what is missing in diligence or who said what.

IC votes live in WhatsApp

The decision is a screenshot. A year later nobody can show who voted what, and why.

Portfolio numbers are chased every quarter

Partners email founders for revenue, burn and runway and retype the answers into a sheet.

LP reports are rebuilt by hand

Every quarter the same tables are rebuilt, and an LP asking “where is this number from?” waits a week.

Compliance dates sit in someone’s head

SEBI filings and capital calls are remembered, not owned.

The system starts empty

A CRM that waits to be filled never gets filled. Command is fed by Atlas and by the founders’ own data rooms.

Six aha moments to remember

1 · The portfolio fills itself

A founder updates their Ascent data room; revenue, burn, runway, headcount and ARR appear in Command tagged “Synced · Ascent”. The Quarterly Pack shows the source and month of every figure.

2 · Won means everything follows

Marking a deal Won creates the portfolio company and links its term sheet, diligence and IC votes.

3 · IC votes need no account

Each member votes from a personal link and cannot see the others. The archive keeps who voted what, and why.

4 · “Needs you today”

Kai flags a deal that has not moved in 13 days, a quiet LP, an overdue task, without being asked.

5 · LP reporting in clicks

LP report, shareable views, briefings, a questionnaire drafted only from the fund’s own documents, a waterfall, and TVPI, DPI and IRR computed live.

6 · The compliance calendar

Eighteen dated SEBI obligations for the next twelve months, and one click, Add 18 to Tasks.

A deal from first look to exit

Follow one company through the fund. All names here are illustrative.

01
Source Intelligence > Thesis matches
A founder, Sunbrook Energy, makes rooftop solar with batteries for small factories. It shows up in Thesis matches with green Sector fit, Stage fit and Geography fit chips, because the founder opted in through Ascent. Or the company arrives through the fund’s public apply link, scored against the thesis, or from a Connect scouting campaign. The partner clicks Express interest; the founder decides whether to reply.
02
First look Deals > Pipeline
The associate types the name and clicks Add to First Look. In the deal drawer they click AI Screen, Draft Memo and Seen before?. Kai drafts; the associate edits, clicks Save Memo, and the card shows IC memo on file.
03
Meeting Deals > Pipeline, Operations > Activity log
The card moves to Meeting with Meeting →. The calls and emails are logged as activities against the deal.
04
Diligence Deals > Diligence tracker
The card moves to Diligence. Start checklist opens financial, legal, market, product and team items. Each is Not started, In progress, Complete or Flagged. Eight of nine complete is visible on the card.
05
Term sheet Deals > Term sheet tracker
The deal moves to Term Sheet. The tracker holds the amount and valuation, the status (Issued, Negotiating, Signed) and the economics: pre-money, liquidation preference, pro-rata rights, option pool, board seats.
06
Investment committee Deals > IC voting
The partner clicks Create vote links. Each member opens a personal link, votes Yes, No or Abstain with a reason, and cannot see the others. The result card reads “Majority yes (2-0, 1 abstain)”. The IC-ready report prints on one page.
07
Closed Won Deals > Pipeline
The partner clicks Won. The portfolio company is created and linked with its term sheet, diligence and IC votes. The signed term sheet seeds the cap table. An automatic note in the activity log says so. The partner clicks Invite company; the founder accepts and builds their own Ascent room.
08
Owning it Portfolio > Metrics, Monitoring, Board and calls
Revenue, burn, runway, headcount and ARR arrive tagged Synced · Ascent. The Monitoring worklist says who owes an update; Ask for update sends the reminder. Generate with AI prepares a board brief from the company’s own numbers. A follow-on reserve is set aside.
09
Valuing and reporting Portfolio > Valuation; Investors > Quarterly pack, LP report
Each quarter a valuation is saved. NAV, TVPI, DPI and IRR update at once. The Quarterly Pack shows every figure with its source and month. The LP report and shareable view go out.
10
Exit and return Operations > Capital calls and distributions; Investors > Distribution waterfall
When the company is sold, the proceeds are recorded and the company moves from unrealized to realized in Fund performance. The distribution is issued to LPs, tracked LP by LP, and split between LPs and the GP by the waterfall using the fund’s own terms. DPI rises, and the Financial story now carries the proof.

One data room per fund vehicle; the first is included. It holds the fund’s own answers in ten sections and 169 fields. Outcomes: LPs and regulators get one consistent set of answers; the strategy answers drive thesis matching; the readiness ring in the sidebar shows what is still open; teammates are invited as Viewer or Editor. A second vehicle gets its own room, sharing the firm’s team and documents.

11 Plans and who pays

Command is sold by conversation. The only prices to quote are the ones that belong to the other products.

How access works

Command is invite-only while LabelNest onboards fund managers directly. There is no self-serve checkout. Access and the plan are granted by LabelNest staff; anyone without it sees Request Access. Funds are set up with the team. A demo plan exists for demonstrations.

What is included

The first fund data room. A number of included seats for founders invited into Ascent. Companies only tracked, with no invitation, never use a seat. Associates and analysts are added as team members with roles.

Who pays

The fund. Founders invited by a fund use Ascent: it starts with a free 30-day pass, then a 12-month plan at ₹24,999 plus GST, with a pay-upfront or pay-later option, or opt out. Connect is free for investors. Atlas has a free tier of 25 credits and paid plans.
