The three hubs

Ascent is the hub for founders (everyone reads the room). Command is the hub for funds (everyone reaches Sentinel through it). Atlas is the hub for data (it feeds every other module).

AscentCommandAtlas

Connect is the single home

Investors, mentors, legal firms and scouts all live in Connect. Other modules link to it and never copy its pages, so there is always one place to look.

Connect

Sentinel is the quiet end

It sits at the end of the chain and only scores. It does not push anything back, and it never shows the data that improved a score.

Sentinel
