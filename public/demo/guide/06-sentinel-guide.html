01 The promise in one minute

Two products, two looks, one wordmark: a calm, sober, editorial feel that suits people who work with money and risk.

Next on the roadmap
Sentinel is in early access. It is the next product in the NestLens family, with live public pages, a sign-in and a "Request access" button. Access is by request: the LabelNest team sets up a workspace, and the customer's admin adds their people. This guide promises no dates and no extra features. 02 Who buys it, and who uses it

The buyer is a fund, an investor or an institution. The daily user is an analyst, a risk or compliance person, or an engineer.

Who buys

Funds and PE firms

VC, PE and growth managers with private companies in the portfolio. They want to see concentration, stale marks and slow exits before the LP asks.

Sentinel Risk

Limited partners and family offices

They hold commitments across several managers and need to ask better questions about marks and exposure.

Sentinel Risk

Institutions and banks

Large allocators and banks that need risk views at scale and, for AI, a governed register of every system.

RiskAI

Enterprises that use AI

Technology, risk and compliance teams running AI that talks to customers, processes documents or makes decisions.

Sentinel AI

Funds and fintechs that use AI

Lenders and funds are both: they hold portfolios and run AI. They can use both halves with one sign-in.

RiskAI

Audit firms and platforms

Regulators, audit firms and platform vendors are named as partners on the AI page.

04 The journey from first touch to value

Both products follow the same shape: add what you hold or run, score it, read the reason, send a report.

Sentinel Risk, from first touch to value

Stage 1Read

Gateway and /risk. Five checks, one example result.

Stage 2Request access

Workspace set up for the firm.

Stage 3Add the portfolio

Funds, then companies: one by one, pasted or from a CSV.

Stage 4Refresh scores

One button runs all five checks for the fund in view.

Stage 5Read Home

What needs you today, the five checks, alerts.

Stage 6Open a check

Score, level, three plain lines, inputs.

Stage 7Send a report

Portfolio report, or LP, committee or board summary. Print or save as PDF.

Sentinel AI, from first touch to value

Stage 1Read

Gateway and /ai. Seven areas, one example result.

Stage 2Request access

Workspace set up for the company.

Stage 3Add a system

Five short steps, in plain words.

Stage 4Assess it

Scores the system across the seven areas.

Stage 5Read the audit

Ring, three weakest areas, area-by-area findings.

Stage 6Fix first

Overview ranks the weakest five systems with the step that helps most.

Stage 7Send the report

Per-system audit and whole-estate report for the board.

Aha moment
For a fund: the first time the analyst opens Concentration and reads "Technology is 41% of what you hold. The usual limit for one sector is 40%." For AI: the first time the CTO sees the Fix first list put the weakest system on top.

Concentration (CRE)Is too much riding on one bet?Sector, stage, country and shared co-investors against safe thresholdsScore, largest sector against its limit, concentration index, breakdown bars, a flag when crossed
Valuation (VIE)Does the manager's mark hold up?Each mark against an independent estimate from public comparables, adjusted for illiquidityA table with divergence, write-down risk and Flagged or Clean; a per-company explanation
Exit timing (LERE)How long are holdings sitting?Holding periods, chance of an exit in 12, 24 and 36 months, and what a delay does to distributionsScore, three DPI cases, J-curve stage, exit chances per company
Stress test (PSTE)What would a repeat of a bad year do?Preset shocks: a rate rise, a credit squeeze, sector disruption, a 2008-style recessionNAV impact for the portfolio and by company, labelled a simulation
Operations (OTRM)Which companies are quietly struggling?Hiring freezes, key departures, press and revenue signalsA health reading per company, healthy, plateauing or distressed, and a signal log

The portfolio index

One number for the report

The cover of the portfolio report shows an overall index: the average of the concentration, valuation and exit scores. Higher is safer. A radar chart shows the three side by side.
