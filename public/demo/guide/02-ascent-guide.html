The problem it solves

Investor-ready information is scattered. The deck is in one place, the numbers are in a spreadsheet, the legal papers are in email. Founders retype the same company details for every programme and every investor. And nobody tells them how ready they are, or what an investor is about to ask.

Say it
Ascent is where a founder builds the data room once, gets told exactly how investor-ready it is, and shares only what they choose. The same room then feeds every application, every programme and, after funding, the fund that invested.

Who buys and who uses

Founders, pre-seed to Series B and beyondCo-founders and advisers invited to the roomIncubator and accelerator admins (cohort console)Funds whose portfolio reports through it (via Command)

Aha moment
The six moments buyers remember: (1) a 92 out of 100 “Investor-ready” score with ticks and a plain list of blockers; (2) a deck checked against the room, line by line; (3) an Investor Questions sheet written from their own numbers; (4) Financial Story, numbers typed once and a year-over-year chart with drill-down for investors; (5) one switch, Listed or Not listed; (6) after funding, one button publishes a numbered report and the fund sees what changed.

03 The journey from sign-up to value

Ten stages. Each one has a screen below.

Stage 1Sign up

One NestLens account. Ascent has no login of its own. The free 30 Day Pass gives full access while it lasts.

Stage 2Three-question wizard

Company name and funding stage; sector; geography. A sector adds its own section (FinTech adds 13 fields in a zone of their own).

Stage 3Fill the room

Any order. Import from a template, a pitch deck or a filled template. Link documents from the vault.

Stage 4Watch readiness climb

Fix blockers, check the Ready for ticks, take the self-assessment.

Stage 5Prepare

Check the deck against the room, prepare Investor Questions, tell the Financial Story.

Stage 6Find capital

Matches, open fund theses, Be found. New rooms are listed by default and marked as raising; one click turns it off.

Stage 7Share and track

Make an investor view with NDA; watch Analytics.

Stage 8Raise

Investors on Connect send interest; the founder accepts or declines in Interest Requests.

Stage 9Funded

Set Funding status to Funded, ask LabelNest to unlock the post-funding fields, publish numbered reports.

Stage 10Day 35 decision

Pay upfront, pay later, or opt out.

Say it
Walk the founder along this rail. Every stage has one screen and one outcome. Never start at the end: start with the wizard and the first percentage.

A section drawer. Back to all sections, paging 1 / 16, the percentage and count, the “What might investors ask?” link and one row per field with its type, value, source line, visibility and status. (Illustration in the product’s style, with example data.)

What you see

Each field row shows the name, a Required tag, the type, the value, a source line (for example “Manual, updated 13d ago”), a visibility selector, a fixed tag “Investor sees Pre-Seed+” (the stage from which investors see it), History, a status dropdown and Fill.

What the founder does

  1. Click a row and type or pick an answer.
  2. Click the status dot to cycle Complete, In Progress, Not Started, N/A.
  3. Choose visibility: Open in shared link, Gated (request access) or Hidden from viewers.
  4. Open History to see every change, then link a vault document if the field takes a file.

Why it matters

Visibility is set per field, not per folder. The founder shows an investor exactly what they want, and the score and every investor-facing thing read from this one room.

Connects to

Document Vault (link a file), Share with Investors (visibility is respected on every link), Readiness (status feeds the score).

Aha moment
“What might investors ask?” on every section gives five to eight questions grounded in what is filled in, or missing, in that very section. Coaching arrives at the moment of filling, not in a separate report.

17 What a Growth Partner says in the first meeting

A short script, discovery questions and the answers to the objections you will hear.

Your job in the first meeting is not to explain every screen. It is to find the founder’s stage and show them one moment of value.

Say it
“Tell me where you are: pre-seed, raising now, or already funded? Today I will show you a single room that investors, programmes and your future investors can all read, without you retyping anything.”

Discovery questions

  1. Where are your investor documents today, and how many places?
  2. How many programmes or investors have asked you for the same details?
  3. Do you know how ready an investor would call you, in a number?
  4. Have you ever shared a link and had no idea if it was opened?
  5. If you close a round, who tells your investors how you are doing?

What you do in the room

  1. Open Ascent home, then the Data Room. Show the 16-section grid and the Funding status control.
  2. Open one section; point at the blue investor question and the visibility selector.
  3. Show the Readiness score and the Ready for ticks.
  4. Show Find capital: your best 10, then the Be found card and the Listed switch.
  5. End on the free 30 Day Pass: “Full access today, decide on Day 35.”
