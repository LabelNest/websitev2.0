What Atlas is

One place that holds eight kinds of record and the links between them: Capital Managers (the firms that run funds), Capital Providers (the allocators that back them), Investment Funds, Capital Seekers (companies raising money), Ecosystem Partners (legal, audit, advisory), Ecosystem Builders (accelerators and studios), Market Facilitators (banks, brokers, placement agents), People and Events.

The problem it solves

Finding who to talk to (which allocator, which fund, which person) is slow. The facts sit in registers, news stories and spreadsheets. The big global tools are priced for the biggest institutions. Atlas puts the facts in one structured record and prices it so a first-time manager or a founder can use it.

11 A 10-minute demo run

Five aha moments, in order. Use a real prospect’s firm name if they allow it, otherwise a well-known public one. Do not press Unlock, Summarize, Buy credits or Purchase in a demo unless you mean to spend.

  1. 0:00 The guest page. Open the public Capital Managers page. Names and cities are open; money columns say “Sign in to view”. “This is the free taste.”
  2. 1:30 Home and the command bar. Press Ctrl K, type a firm. “One box for firms, funds and people.” Open it.
  3. 3:00 The profile. Walk the header, the KPI strip, then the Funds, Portfolio and People tabs. Point at the Intel Score and the freshness dot.
  4. 4:30 The relationship explorer. Open Relationships. Click a Layer 1 node, press Next layer, then go to layer three. “Manager, fund, allocator. This is the map.”
  5. 6:00 Markets. Read the headline and click a register number to land on the filtered list.
  6. 7:00 Studio. Ask a plain-English question and show the plan. “Nothing is charged until Run.” Show the price chips.
  7. 8:00 Workspace. Add to list, Track a firm, and open Compare with two firms.
  8. 9:00 Connect the dots. Show a fund page with the Ascent banner, mention Command’s Intelligence tab and Exchange for the data itself.
  9. 9:45 Close. “Start with the free tier of 25 credits. Which three firms would you like to see first?”
If you only have 5 minutes
Do steps 2, 3 and 4, then close. The relationship explorer is what they will remember.
