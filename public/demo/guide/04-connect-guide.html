02 Who uses Connect

Six kinds of people, each with their own front door. Connect is the single home: other NestLens products link into it and never copy its pages.

👩‍💼
The founder
Early-stage, wants a mentor on fundraising and an introduction to a seed fund. Free to browse and to apply.
🧑‍💻
The angel or small fund associate
Wants only companies that fit “SaaS, Deep Tech, Seed, India, Southeast Asia”. Deal Flow is free.
⚖️
The company secretary or lawyer
Wants steady inbound from founders and sets prices per service.
🧑‍🏫
The operator-mentor
Sets session types, duration and price, and gets booked by founders.
🔭
The scout
Sources deals for a fund. Posts a thesis, reviews applicants, uses their own email wording.
🛠️
LabelNest operations
Approves partners, reviews funding applications and prepares founders’ applications with permission.

09 The listing rules: consent without friction

A company is visible to investors because the founder let it be. The default is on, the control is one click, and investors only ever see real companies.

🟢

New data rooms are listed by default

When a founder opens a new Ascent data room, it is listed on Connect and marked as raising. No extra tick box and no email is needed for this.

🔘

One click turns it off

The Listed switch in the Ascent top bar, and the notice “Your company is listed on Connect” with Keep it on and Turn off. A founder who opts out stays out.

🏢

Investors only see real companies

A room is shown to investors only once it has a company name that is not a person’s own name or an email address, plus a sector, a stage or some content.

🔐

Contact stays hidden

Investors see a short summary and, after they accept the NDA, the investor view. Contact details open only after the founder accepts the investor’s interest.
